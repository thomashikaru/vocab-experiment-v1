Data Collection Notice

This experiment is designed to study typing behavior under vocabulary constraints. We will collect the following data:

  • Your typed responses to writing prompts
  • Keystroke timing and efficiency data
  • Word-level typing patterns and corrections

Your data will be used for research purposes only and will be stored securely. No personal information beyond your participant ID will be collected.
