Vocabulary Constraint Experiment

Data Collection Notice

This experiment is designed to study typing behavior under vocabulary constraints. We will collect the following data:

  • Your typed responses to questions
  • Keystroke timing data
  • A final debrief question

No personal information beyond your participant ID will be collected. Your anonymous data will be analyzed for research purposes and may be presented in academic publications and conferences or shared with other researchers.

Instructions

  • This study tests your ability to answer questions using only a limited set of words.
  • You won't be able to type words outside of the vocabulary, and you'll hear a buzzer if you try.
  • It's OK if you don't know the correct answer to the question; just make your best guess.
  • Type naturally rather than choosing words from the dropdown menu. The dropdown menu will show which words are allowed, but it is not clickable.
